## About Me

I'm a master's student at the University of Tokyo, and I focus on the physics of financial markets. Alongside my research, I build independent projects in financial modeling.

I share my academic research and independent projects here.

## Research

I study how cryptocurrency prices move together and how these relationships change over time. I use time series analysis and network science to identify patterns in the market and explore ways to build more diversified portfolios.

## Independent Projects

### [On-chain Risk Intelligence Agent](https://github.com/DINGMINZHAO/onchain-risk-agent)

A Bitcoin transaction risk analysis project combining machine learning, graph analysis, and an LLM agent for investigating suspicious transactions.

### [Portfolio Risk & Model Validation](https://github.com/DINGMINZHAO/portfolio-risk-validation)

An independent evaluation of portfolio strategies, focusing on risk measurement, model backtesting, and stress testing.

### [Credit Risk Decision Engine](https://github.com/DINGMINZHAO/credit-risk-decision-engine)
